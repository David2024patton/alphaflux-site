## THE FLUXCONTENT PRODUCTION LINE

**H2:** How Pieces Go From Brief to Published.

1. **Brief.** Keyword and intent research, outline, and a one-line job for the piece.
2. **Draft.** AI produces the first pass at scale; nothing ships without editing.
3. **Human edit.** A senior editor checks voice, accuracy, claims, and structure.
4. **Optimize.** On-page SEO, internal links, schema, and meta land with the piece.
5. **Measure.** Every piece carries a KPI, and the monthly loop promotes or retires
   it based on the numbers.
