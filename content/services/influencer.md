## MICRO VERSUS MACRO

**H2:** The Reach You Can Measure Beats the Reach You Rent.

A macro creator gives you impressions; a micro creator gives you trust. The
math is not about follower counts. It is about fit, authenticity, and whether
the audience acts. AlphaFlux vets creators on audience quality, engagement
authenticity, and content fit before a single brief ships, and we measure every
partnership against pipeline, not impressions. The creator who moves 200 engaged
buyers is worth more than the one who shows a million passive views. That is the
difference between renting an audience and building a partnership.
