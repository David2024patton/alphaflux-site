## ASO VERSUS PAID ACQUISITION

Organic Installs Are the Margin You Never Pay For.

Paid acquisition fills the funnel today; ASO fills it forever. A dollar of paid
spend buys an install. A ranking keyword buys installs for as long as the ranking
holds. The compounding play is the mix: paid validates demand and buy time, ASO
captures the demand that already exists. Most apps treat the store listing as a
formality. The apps that win treat it as a landing page with a conversion rate,
and they test it like one. That is the difference between an app that grows on
budget and an app that grows on system.


## The Baseline That Decides the Program

ASO starts with a baseline, because you cannot improve what you have not measured. The scorecard captures three things: where your app ranks for the keywords that matter, how much of the search demand you cover, and how the listing converts. Most apps discover a surprise in that first pass. Either they rank for the wrong keywords, or they rank well and convert poorly, or both. The fix order follows the data. If conversion is the leak, listing and visual changes come first, because ranking higher with a weak listing just spends the traffic. If coverage is the leak, keyword strategy and new listing content come first. The program then runs in testable batches: a title change here, a screenshot set there, each shipped with the reasoning attached and measured against the baseline. Reviews sit underneath all of it, because rating and review velocity are part of the ranking signal and the conversion signal. We manage review strategy ethically: asking happy users at the right moment, never buying or faking. That is how an app store presence turns into a predictable install channel.
