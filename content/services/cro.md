## HOW THE PROGRAM RUNS

Test. Learn. Ship. Repeat.

1. **Audit (Days 0-14).** Behavior data plus a conversion teardown of your highest
   traffic pages. We rank opportunities by projected revenue, not opinion.
2. **Roadmap (Month 1).** The first test slate ships. Every test has a hypothesis,
   a metric, and a decision rule.
3. **Iterate (Ongoing).** Winners roll out, losers get retired, learnings get
   written down. FluxData tracks lift and statistical confidence.
4. **Report.** Monthly: tests run, results, revenue impact, and what we test next.
