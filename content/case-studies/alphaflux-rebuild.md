## The Challenge

AlphaFlux's own website was down: the live index.php served a PHP parse error (unclosed bracket on line 146), returning HTTP 200 with a broken body. A marketing agency with a broken website is the failure case the whole company is built against. The rebuild had to be a full site, not a landing page, with every competitor feature and none of the competitor failures.

## Channel Mix (What Shipped)

- 20 service pages across Earned, Paid, Owned, and Specialized pillars
- 9 industry verticals, including Higher Education
- Transparent pricing page with published tiers
- Case study library, resource hub, lead magnet (Free AI Visibility Audit)
- Full SEO: per-page schema, sitemap, robots, GA4 (property in admin settings)
