## PAIN / SOLUTION

Three Problems Franchise Networks Hit, and How We Solve Them.

**1. Franchisees run their own local marketing, and quality varies by market.**
One location runs a great Google profile; another is invisible. The brand pays
for both. We build corporate-owned systems: city x service pages, Google
Business Profile management, and local assets that every location runs
consistently, without making franchisees marketing experts.

**2. Duplicate local pages get filtered, so the network ranks for nothing.**
Most networks copy one template across markets, and search engines ignore the
duplicates. Our local content is templated but unique per location, tuned to
each market's search demand, so every store can win its own map pack instead
of competing with the corporate page.

**3. Nobody can see network performance in one place.**
When location performance lives in separate spreadsheets and dashboards,
underperforming markets hide. We run a network dashboard with every location,
every KPI: rankings, reviews, calls, and revenue, visible to corporate and
franchisees alike.

**How the franchise engagement runs:** the free AI visibility audit maps where
your network wins and where it leaks, market by market. We then roll out the
system centrally and report against location-level KPIs every month. Scope and
setup are published on the pricing page.
