## PAIN / SOLUTION

Three Problems Ecommerce Brands Hit, and How We Solve Them.

**1. Traffic without conversion is just cost.**
Most ecommerce marketing buys clicks and hopes the storefront converts. We
treat CRO as a first-class channel: product pages, checkout, and mobile flows
are rebuilt and tested as part of the growth plan, so every visit carries more
revenue and every paid dollar works harder.

**2. Ad dependence erodes margin as bidding gets more expensive.**
Brands that grow on paid alone watch acquisition cost climb every quarter. We
run search, shopping, and organic as one system, with category SEO and buying
guides carrying demand that paid no longer needs to buy. The mix shifts as
organic weight compounds.

**3. DTC and marketplace channels fight for budget with separate teams.**
When storefront and marketplace operate in silos, you lose margin to
double-spend and cannibalized bids. We plan Amazon, Walmart, Instacart, and
your storefront against one budget and one set of KPIs, so every channel
knows its role in revenue per session.

**How the ecommerce engagement runs:** the free AI visibility audit maps who
wins your category's searches and AI answers today. We then tune the feed,
category architecture, paid mix, and retention flows, and report against
revenue per session and LTV every month. The plan is on the page before you
sign.
