## PAIN / SOLUTION

**H2:** Three Problems B2B Teams Hit, and How We Solve Them.

**1. Marketing is judged on leads, but the deals happen quarters later.**
Most B2B attribution stops at the first touch, so spend drifts toward whatever
generates the most form fills rather than what actually moves the pipeline. We
track every channel to pipeline and revenue: SQLs, deal stages, and
influenced-revenue, reported in the metrics your leadership already uses. When
marketing and sales read the same numbers, budget follows what closes.

**2. Buyers validate vendors in AI engines before they ever contact you.**
ChatGPT and Perplexity now answer "who is the best vendor for X" from a handful
of sources. If your brand is not among them, you are not in the conversation
when the shortlist forms. FluxGEO builds the citations, entity context, and
answer-grade content that make your company the named answer, not a missing
footnote.

**3. Committee decisions stall when sales and marketing disagree on numbers.**
Marketing reports clicks; sales reports closed revenue; nobody trusts the
middle. We run one dashboard with one metric set for both teams, so the debate
moves from whose data is right to which channel gets the next dollar.

**How the B2B engagement runs:** the free AI visibility audit maps who answers
