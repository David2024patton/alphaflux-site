## PAIN / SOLUTION

Three Problems Law Firms Hit, and How We Solve Them.

**1. Legal advertising rules vary by state bar, and the risk is real.**
A claim that is fine in one jurisdiction can be a problem in another. We build
with state bar advertising and disclosure rules in mind and route every claim
through your review before publication, so the marketing moves without the
ethics anxiety.

**2. Clients research the firm and the attorney before they ever call.**
The decision to hire is made in the research phase: practice area pages,
reviews, and increasingly AI answers about which firm to trust. We build the
authority content, local city x practice area pages, and FluxGEO citations
that make your firm the answer when the research happens.

**3. Intake quality decides firm economics.**
A firm can buy all the calls in the world and still lose money if the intakes
are the wrong matters at the wrong price point. We scope paid and landing
architecture around case intake value, so budget flows to the practice areas
and jurisdictions that pay for the firm.

**How the legal engagement runs:** the free AI visibility audit maps who
answers for your practice area today. We then build the authority, local, and
reputation systems around your footprint, and report against intake and cost
per acquisition every month. Pricing is published on the page.
