## PAIN / SOLUTION

Three Problems Institutions Hit, and How We Solve Them.

**1. Program pages read like catalogs and rank for nothing.**
Institutional descriptions answer the school's questions, not the student's.
We rebuild program pages around what prospective students actually search:
outcomes, curriculum fit, cost, and comparison. Every program gets a page
built to rank for the demand that drives applications.

**2. Students ask AI engines for recommendations, and your institution is absent.**
"Which program is best for X" is now a ChatGPT and Perplexity query, answered
from a short list of cited sources. FluxGEO builds the citations, entity
context, and answer-grade content that put your programs in those answers
instead of leaving the recommendation to a competitor's page.

**3. Admissions teams drown in inquiries that are not qualified fits.**
A high inquiry count means nothing if the fit is wrong. We scope content and
paid around program fit and funnel stage, and track inquiries, applications,
and enrollment cost, so the funnel fills with students who are likely to
enroll, not just click.

**How the higher ed engagement runs:** the free AI visibility audit maps who
answers for your programs today. We then build the program, campus, and
enrollment content systems around your admissions rhythm, and report against
inquiries and applications every month. Pricing is published on the page.
