## The Short Answer

Prospects check your reviews before they check your price. Reputation management
is the system that makes your best reviews the ones they see first: more volume
from happy customers, fast and human responses to the rest, and the right
reviews surfaced in search.

## Why Local Reputation Decides the Sale

For local services, the review profile is the sales page. A business with 200
reviews and a 4.8 wins the click over a business with 12 reviews and a 5.0,
because volume is trust. A business that responds to criticism looks accountable;
a business that stays silent looks guilty. Neither outcome is an accident.

## The Review System, in Five Parts

### 1. Volume: ask at the right moment

The best review request is the one that arrives when the customer is happiest:
after a successful service, a delivered job, a resolved ticket. Ask happy
customers at that moment, through a channel they already use.

### 2. Ethics: never buy, never fake

Buying or incentivizing reviews violates platform rules and, increasingly, the
law. One solicited 5-star review from a fake account can cost you the whole
profile. Do not risk the asset for a number.

### 3. Response: every review gets an answer

Response rate is visible to prospects and weighted by platforms. A fast,
specific, human response to a complaint converts onlookers better than a perfect
rating with silence.

### 4. Visibility: surface the best reviews

Review schema and local SEO make your ratings appear in search results. The
reviews prospects see before they click are the ones you optimize.

### 5. Monitoring: know what is being said

Weekly sentiment and volume tracking catches problems early, celebrates wins,
and feeds your content and service teams.

## The Response Framework

- **Positive review:** thank, name the specifics, invite the next visit.
- **Mixed review:** acknowledge the valid point, state the fix, thank them.
- **Negative review:** apologize for the experience, move to a private channel,
  describe the correction. Never argue publicly, never get defensive.

## Reputation Meets Local SEO

Ratings schema and Google Business Profile signals feed local ranking directly.
A strong review profile is not just trust; it is a ranking factor. The two
systems reinforce each other, which is why reputation and local SEO run together.

## The Starting Audit (Do This This Week)

1. Pull your current rating, review count, and response rate.
2. Compare against your top three local competitors.
3. Check whether your reviews appear in search results with schema.
4. Set a volume target: how many reviews a month moves you from 20 to 100.

## FAQ

**Can you make bad reviews disappear?**

No, and no honest agency will try. We build volume and visibility so your best
reviews surface first, and we respond to criticism in a way that converts
onlookers.

**Is it ethical to ask customers for reviews?**

Yes, when done right: asking happy customers at the right moment, never
incentivizing specific ratings, and following platform rules.

**Does reputation affect SEO?**

Yes. Ratings schema and Google Business Profile signals feed local SEO
directly.

**How fast can review volume grow?**

With a request system in place, most local businesses can move from single
digits to 20+ reviews per month within a quarter.
