## The Short Answer

GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, is the practice of making your brand the
source that AI answer engines cite when they answer buyer questions. Where SEO
optimizes for rankings in a list of links, GEO optimizes for being quoted inside
the synthesized answer itself.

## Why It Matters Now

More buyers now start research in AI chat: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and
Google AI Overviews. When they ask "which product or service should I choose",
the engine writes an answer from a handful of sources. If your brand is not among
them, you are invisible at the exact moment the buying decision forms. This is
not a future trend. It is current search behavior, and it is compounding.

## How AI Engines Choose Sources

Answer engines weigh several signals when deciding who to cite:

1. **Entity consistency.** Is your brand described the same way everywhere?
2. **Structured data.** Can the engine parse your pages as data, not just text?
3. **Source authority.** Do trusted publications and domains cite you?
4. **Direct answers.** Does your content answer the question plainly, in
   quotable form?
5. **Freshness.** Is your information current, or stale?

## The GEO Playbook (What Actually Moves Citations)

### 1. Fix the entity layer first

Name, address, logo, and description must be consistent across your site,
profiles, and directories. Engines cannot cite a brand they cannot identify.

### 2. Add structured data

Organization, Service, FAQ, and Review schema give engines a parseable picture
of what you offer and what others say about you.

### 3. Write quotable content

Answer direct questions directly. A clear, one-paragraph answer to "what is your
pricing model" is worth more to an AI engine than a clever landing page.

### 4. Build source authority

Digital PR, guest contributions, and earned coverage in trusted publications
signal that your domain is a source engines can rely on.

### 5. Monitor your citations

Track where you appear, where competitors appear, and what changed, weekly.
You cannot improve what you do not measure.

## GEO vs SEO: The One-Paragraph Difference

SEO wins the ranking; GEO wins the citation inside the answer. They are not
rivals. They compound: a page that ranks well and is structured for extraction
wins both surfaces. That is why the AlphaFlux stack runs FluxSEO and FluxGEO
together.

## Common Mistakes

- **Chasing the AI answer itself.** You do not control what an engine says; you
  control the signals it weighs. Anyone promising to "make ChatGPT say X" is
  selling something.
- **Ignoring structured data.** The easiest wins are the ones engines can parse.
- **Treating GEO as a one-time project.** Citations decay. Monitoring and
  authority building are ongoing.

## The 90-Day Arc

- Days 0-14: baseline visibility score and citation gap analysis.
- Days 15-45: entity and structured data fixes.
- Days 30-90: quotable content and authority building.
- Month 3+: monitoring, iteration, and compounding.
