## The Short Answer

AI search optimization is the work of making your brand visible, cited, and
recommended in AI answer engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI
Overviews. It is GEO in action, applied surface by surface, with monitoring and
campaigns where the platforms allow.

## Why Brands Need an AI Search Playbook

Traditional search gave you a list of links and a chance. AI search gives the
buyer a synthesized answer, often before any link appears. The brand cited in
that answer inherits the trust of the recommendation. The brands absent from it
are not competing; they are not in the conversation at all.

## The Surfaces, Explained

| Surface | How Buyers Use It | What Matters There |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------|
| ChatGPT | Validation, comparison, "best X" questions | Cited sources, entity consistency |
| Perplexity | Research-style queries with source citations | Source authority, quoted content |
| Gemini | Google ecosystem answers and assistant queries | Structured data, Google signals |
| Google AI Overviews | AI summary atop normal search results | Featured source selection |
| AI commerce | Product recommendations inside shopping answers | Product schema, review signals |

## The Playbook, Surface by Surface

### 1. ChatGPT

- Make your site's core pages directly quotable: pricing, services, FAQ.
- Keep entity details consistent so ChatGPT describes you correctly.
- Consider sponsored AI presence where available (ChatGPT Ads).

### 2. Perplexity

- Earn citations from sources Perplexity trusts: publications, research,
  high-authority domains.
- Publish original data and research; it gets cited.

### 3. Gemini and AI Overviews

- Structured data is non-negotiable: Organization, Service, FAQ, Review.
- Answer the direct question on the page, not just near it.

### 4. AI Commerce

- Product schema, clean feeds, and strong review signals decide whether AI
  shopping answers recommend you.

## The Measurement Problem (And the Fix)

You cannot optimize what you cannot see. The fix is a visibility audit: check
your brand and keywords across the surfaces, score your presence, and track
citation share monthly. Most brands have never run one. That is the opportunity.

## The Campaign Layer

Some AI surfaces now accept advertising. ChatGPT Ads is the emerging example.
Like any paid channel, AI campaigns need planning, measurement, and client-owned
accounts. Treat them as a new surface with new rules, not a bolt-on.

## The 5 Mistakes Brands Make

1. Assuming Google rankings transfer to AI answers. They help, but citation is a
   separate surface.
2. Ignoring structured data. Engines cannot cite what they cannot parse.
3. Writing for humans only. Answer the question plainly; quotable wins.
4. Never monitoring. Citation share changes weekly; blind brands get displaced.
5. Believing anyone can "control" what an AI says. You engineer signals, not
   outputs.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you really control what AI engines say about a brand?**

A: No one controls outputs. You engineer the signals engines weigh: entity
consistency, structured data, authority, quotable content. That measurably moves
citation share.

**Q: How is AI search optimization different from GEO?**

A: GEO is the citation science; AI search optimization is the applied campaign
work across specific engines, including paid AI presence. They run together.

**Q: Do I need this if I rank on Google?**

A: If your buyers ask AI first, yes. AI answers are replacing the first click
for a growing share of demand.

**Q: How do I start?**

A: Run a visibility audit across the major engines. The report shows your
baseline, your gaps, and the fix list.
